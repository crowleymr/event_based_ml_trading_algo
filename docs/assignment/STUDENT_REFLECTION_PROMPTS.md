# Student reflection prompts

This file deliberately contains prompts, not completed reflection. Answers must be written by the student from first-hand review and experience.

- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — understanding:** Explain the end-to-end system without looking at the architecture diagram. Which boundary was hardest to understand?
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — practical challenge:** Describe a concrete issue you personally reviewed or resolved. What evidence showed the resolution was correct?
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — algorithm choice:** Why are Elastic Net and histogram GBT informative comparators for this task? What trade-off do you see?
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — leakage:** Give your own example of how filed dates, labels or preprocessing could leak future information.
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — metric judgment:** Why might MAE/RMSE, IC and Sharpe disagree? Which disagreement appears in the generated evidence?
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — AI critique:** Identify one AI-assisted proposal you accepted, changed or rejected. Explain the evidence and your reasoning.
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — independent verification:** State what code, tests, tables or plots you personally checked and what you learned.
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — knowledge gap:** Name a concept you still cannot confidently explain. Record the source or experiment you will use to close it.
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — limits:** Which claim would you refuse to make from this study, and why?
- **STUDENT TO COMPLETE — future decision:** Choose one next research step and justify why it should precede more complex modelling.
